About the artist

Art by Chitrangda Goyal

Hi! I'm Chitrangda Goyal, and I've been creating mandala art for several years now — each piece is built by hand on canvas, fabric, or paper, dot by dot. Mandala art isn't just a craft for me, it's a way of finding focus and balance.

You'll find pieces here in a range of sizes, colours, and prices — something for every budget. Reach out if you'd like to commission a custom mandala too.
